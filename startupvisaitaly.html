Startup Criteria

Not every startup qualifies. To direct your €250,000 into the innovative startup route, the target company must meet three conditions: it must be officially registered in Italy's innovative startup registry (startup.registroimprese.it), incorporated within the past five years, and generating less than €5 million in annual revenue. The focus is on technology, deep-tech, and innovation-driven ventures — exactly the sector where Italian engineering brilliance intersects with global demand.

Critical 2026 Update

The Flat Tax Just Changed

From 1 January 2026, Italy's flat tax regime for new residents was updated by the 2026 Budget Law. The annual substitute tax on all foreign-sourced income — dividends, interest, rental income, capital gains — is now set at a fixed €300,000 per year, regardless of the amount earned abroad.

Each qualifying family member adds €50,000 per year. The regime lasts up to 15 years. For high-net-worth individuals earning over €1 million per year in foreign income, this represents an extraordinary level of tax efficiency unavailable almost anywhere else in Europe.
